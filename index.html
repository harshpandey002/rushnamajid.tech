01 — About

Good research starts with a survey that asks exactly what it means to and returns data nobody has to second-guess.

That's the part I own. I script questionnaires with their routing, quotas and validation, test them until they break and then until they don't, and look after data quality while the study is in field. When something repeats, I automate it.

I studied Information Technology (diploma, then a BE at Pillai College of Engineering) and joined Ugam straight out of college in 2021. Two promotions later I moved to Acuity Knowledge Partners, where I've worked remotely since 2024.

I'm now looking for my next role: somewhere I can go deeper into data and automation and work with a team that cares about getting the details right.
